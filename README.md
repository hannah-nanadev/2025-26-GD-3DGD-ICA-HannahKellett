# 2025-26-GD-3DGD-ICA-HannahKellett
Escape Room Project

## Overview
You are Closet, a prisoner trapped on a spaceship with a puzzle after being framed for a crime - Should she solve it, she shall be pardoned and allowed to return to Earth.

## How To Play
WASD to move. Move mouse to move camera. Tab to open inventory - Click on items to open a menu with which you can use them on an object you are facing, or examine them for information.
E to interact - Note that the walls tend to eat interaction inputs and error out. I was not able to solve this in good time. The game is still beatable regardless despite the lack of polish.
Many objects in the game have several lines of dialogue. You may have to press E on them multiple times.

## Stage 1 Reflection
I did zero planning until stage 2. I was overburdened by other projects and was not able to start this one until after Stage 2's due date. Apologies.
I had a good time with it, though! I didn't use any references to make an environment that was in any way grounded in reality, but I had the chance to mess with MetaSound a lot, get properly accustomed with blueprints and to make my first real models and animations. I'm amazed with what I accomplished here.
Known bug - the way I run my events internally does not exist if the project is cooked as Shipping. Was not able to find a fix. Please build project as Development instead.
